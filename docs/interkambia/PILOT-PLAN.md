# InterKambia 90-Day Pilot Plan

## Phase 1 — Days 1–30: validate

### Goals

- identify the strongest recurring cross-border problems;
- recruit the first provider and client cohort;
- resolve the most important legal and operating assumptions;
- avoid building transactional automation before evidence.

### Work

1. Create provider and client interview scripts.
2. Interview 15 Curaçao providers.
3. Interview 10 Netherlands potential clients.
4. Score demand across the three proposed launch corridors.
5. Recruit five pilot providers and five pilot clients.
6. Complete identity, portfolio, reference, and service-scope review.
7. Prepare direct-contract, milestone, acceptance, cancellation, and service-swap templates.
8. Obtain initial entity, tax, privacy, DAC7, and payment-provider advice.

### Evidence gate

Proceed only when at least one category has repeated demand, credible providers, realistic budgets, and users willing to enter a guided pilot.

## Phase 2 — Days 31–60: concierge pilot

### Goals

- complete five real matches;
- measure operator work, delivery quality, trust issues, and willingness to pay;
- prove the workflow manually before coding it deeply.

### Workflow

```text
Request
→ Qualification
→ Provider shortlist
→ Human match approval
→ Proposal
→ Direct agreement
→ Direct payment or bilateral swap
→ Milestone delivery
→ Acceptance
→ Review
→ Incident and learning log
```

### Pilot operating rules

- invite-only participation;
- B2B transactions only;
- platform does not hold funds;
- operator approves every match;
- no unsupported regulated services;
- no AI-generated legal or tax decisions;
- every match has acceptance criteria and an escalation owner.

### Metrics captured per match

- sourcing time;
- qualification time;
- operator hours;
- proposal cycle time;
- project value;
- delivery duration;
- revisions;
- completion result;
- client and provider satisfaction;
- dispute or incident type;
- repeat intent;
- willingness to pay InterKambia.

## Phase 3 — Days 61–90: product decision

### Build only what the pilot proves

Candidate modules:

- authentication;
- organization and provider profiles;
- service catalog;
- request intake;
- operator qualification;
- matching recommendations;
- proposals and agreements;
- milestones and acceptance;
- reviews and incidents;
- compliance and audit records.

### Decision framework

#### Build

Choose build when demand repeats, five matches complete successfully, users accept a monetization model, and compliance has a feasible path.

#### Revise

Choose revise when one corridor works but the audience, category, pricing, or workflow needs narrowing.

#### Stop

Choose stop when demand is weak, quality cannot be verified, disputes are structurally high, or compliance and payment costs destroy the economics.

## Recommended pilot monetization experiments

Test separately rather than combining all fees:

- free founding cohort;
- provider verification fee;
- monthly provider membership;
- client matching package;
- fixed success fee after completion;
- enterprise sourcing subscription.

Do not commit to a permanent percentage fee until average transaction value, acquisition cost, operator effort, PSP cost, refunds, and disputes are known.

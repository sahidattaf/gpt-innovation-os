# AI Business Transformation Program

The flagship consulting and implementation offer from GPT Innovation by Attaf.

## Client outcome

Help an owner-led business identify high-value AI opportunities, establish operational visibility, implement priority workflows, train the team, and measure results.

## Engagement options

| Package | Best for | Core deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Good — AI Opportunity Scan | Businesses needing direction | Assessment and 90-day roadmap |
| Better — Business Control Center | Businesses needing visibility and control | Notion command center, KPIs, SOPs, and executive assistant |
| Best — AI Business Operating System | Businesses ready to integrate and automate | Application, data layer, agents, integrations, governance, and monthly optimization |

## Standard workflow

1. Qualify the opportunity
2. Run discovery
3. Map the current state
4. Score use cases
5. Present the assessment
6. Approve scope and commercial terms
7. Implement in phases
8. Validate with users
9. Train and hand over
10. Review impact monthly

## Required outputs

- Discovery record
- Current-state workflow map
- Opportunity scorecard
- Executive assessment
- ROI hypotheses
- Proposal and statement of work
- 90-day roadmap
- Implementation and QA checklists
- Training and handover record
- Monthly executive report

## Human approval gates

Human approval is required before:

- Pricing or contractual commitments
- Financial, staffing, or legal decisions
- Sending external client communications
- Production deployment
- Accessing or migrating sensitive client data
- Material changes to an agreed scope

## Data rule

This repository contains reusable templates and non-sensitive configuration only. Client secrets, customer data, employee data, financial records, credentials, and private operational datasets must be stored in approved restricted systems.

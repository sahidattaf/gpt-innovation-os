# Sebier Gas Station Pilot — 30-Day Operating Pack

## Objective

Validate three control workflows at one gas-station location before expanding the pattern to other Sebier business units:

1. Daily cash and POS reconciliation
2. Shift handover
3. Daily executive briefing

This pilot supports operational control. It does not replace the official POS, accounting, banking, safety, or compliance systems.

## Success criteria

- At least 95% of shifts submit a complete handover.
- Reconciliation is completed by the management-approved deadline.
- Every material variance has an owner, explanation, evidence, and status.
- Ownership receives one concise daily briefing.
- All calculations remain traceable to source records.
- No AI workflow approves financial adjustments or consequential staff decisions.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Cashier / shift operator | Count cash, close POS, record incidents, reference evidence |
| Shift supervisor | Review handover and verify known differences |
| Finance / administrator | Compare cash, cards, POS, fleet credit, deposits, and adjustments |
| Station manager | Approve exceptions and assign corrective action |
| Owner | Review executive briefing and approve consequential decisions |
| GPT Innovation | Configure, test, document, train, and evaluate |

## 30-day rollout

### Week 1 — Diagnose and baseline

- Confirm current POS, shift-close, cash-count, deposit, and escalation procedures.
- Define roles, deadlines, variance thresholds, and approval rules.
- Measure current reconciliation time, missing records, and typical discrepancies.

### Week 2 — Configure and test

- Configure the reconciliation, handover, and briefing templates.
- Test with historical or sanitized records.
- Validate every calculation against the current manual process.
- Train pilot users.

### Week 3 — Controlled live pilot

- Run at one location.
- Review exceptions every day.
- Correct missing fields, unclear steps, and alert noise.
- Keep the official accounting process unchanged.

### Week 4 — Stabilize and decide

- Measure completion, timing, variances, adoption, and owner usefulness.
- Document lessons and approved revisions.
- Record a go, improve, or stop decision.

## Human-control requirements

AI may summarize, compare, calculate, classify, and flag. AI must not authorize:

- cash adjustments;
- refunds or write-offs;
- bank deposits;
- supplier payments;
- credit limits;
- disciplinary actions;
- safety or compliance decisions.

## Pilot KPIs

- Reconciliation completion rate
- Average reconciliation time
- Number and value of variances
- Variances resolved within one business day
- Shift-handover completion rate
- Open issues carried between shifts
- Briefing delivery rate
- Corrective actions triggered
- Owner satisfaction

## Expansion gate

Do not expand to car rental, hardware, tires, or property rentals until:

- calculations are validated;
- staff adoption is acceptable;
- exception ownership works;
- the owner confirms the briefing is useful;
- data handling and permissions are approved.

Reuse the control pattern, not gas-station-specific fields.

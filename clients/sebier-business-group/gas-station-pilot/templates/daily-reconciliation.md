## Calculations

- Expected cash = opening float + cash sales − refunds − approved payouts − safe drops
- Cash variance = physical closing cash − expected cash
- POS total = fuel sales + convenience-store sales
- Sales-channel variance = POS total − cash sales − card sales − fleet/credit sales − approved adjustments

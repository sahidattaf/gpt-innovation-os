# Sebier Business Group — Flagship Living Laboratory

## Status

Internal flagship discovery and validation client for the GPT Innovation AI Business Transformation Method.

## Portfolio in scope

- Car rental
- Hardware and building supplies
- Tire sales and service
- Fireworks retail
- Christmas seasonal retail
- Real estate and property rentals
- Gas station and convenience retail

## Objective

Use Sebier Business Group to validate the full consulting lifecycle:

`Discover → Diagnose → Design → Demonstrate → Deploy → Develop`

The goal is to produce measurable improvements for the group while converting public-safe patterns into reusable GPT Innovation assets.

## Architecture rule

Sebier is managed through one executive group layer, but each business unit keeps separate:

- Workflows
- Permissions
- KPIs
- Operational data
- Financial records
- Compliance controls

Cross-business integrations are introduced only after discovery confirms the business benefit, data ownership, security requirements, and management approval.

## Shared executive capabilities

- Group scorecard
- Business-unit dashboards
- Decision and approval queue
- Weekly executive briefing
- Risk and exception register
- Shared supplier, customer, or asset insights where authorized

## Initial discovery sequence

1. Confirm legal entities, locations, owners, and managers.
2. Inventory current software, spreadsheets, WhatsApp flows, paper processes, and data owners.
3. Document one revenue workflow and one control workflow per business unit.
4. Establish baseline KPIs.
5. Score opportunities by value, effort, risk, readiness, and adoption difficulty.
6. Select one 30-day pilot.

## Recommended pilot candidates

- Car rental: reservation intake and fleet availability
- Hardware: inventory and quotation workflow
- Tire sales: product matching and appointment flow
- Real estate: inquiry and maintenance tracking
- Gas station: daily reconciliation, shift handover, and executive briefing

## Data policy

This public repository must contain no customer lists, employee records, bank details, supplier pricing, contracts, credentials, financial statements, or operational security information.

Private evidence and working data belong in restricted Notion pages, Supabase, or other approved client systems.

## Case-study rule

Sebier becomes a public case study only after management approves the scope, claims, metrics, and information to be disclosed.
